A plain classifier read at an upper quantile matches the proposed method's recall with about 30% less over-provisioning, but loses under a 15-minute actuation delay. The train.py from this run was changed later, so it isn't in the history.

The look-ahead alone beats the paper's target at every matched over-provisioning level (+8 points of spike coverage and 19% less throttling at slack 0.6), so holding the allocation after a spike costs more than it helps.
- 2026-10-23: Added a 30-minute look-ahead target to test whether matching the look-ahead to the delay restores coverage at 30 minutes; training started. Set up this repo: portable runners, a setup step that pins the authors' code, and these process files.
